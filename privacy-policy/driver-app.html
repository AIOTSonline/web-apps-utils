Privacy Policy for Powerdrive Learning

Application: Powerdrive Learning Package ID: com.arshina.powerdrive_learning Developer: Arshina IoT Solutions

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 • Last Updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arshina IoT Solutions ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards your personal data when you install, access, or use the Powerdrive Learning mobile application (Package ID: com.arshina.powerdrive_learning), designed for Austrian driving license theory exam preparation.

Google Play Data Safety Commitment: Powerdrive Learning collects only the essential personal data required for user authentication and cross-device learning progress synchronization. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

1. Information Collected in Powerdrive Learning

To deliver an interactive driving theory learning experience and save your study progress, the app collects the following data:

A. User Account Information

  • Email Address: Required to create your account, manage secure authentication, and allow password recovery.
  • Full Name / Display Name: Optional profile information used for in-app personalization.
  • Authentication Tokens: Passwords are encrypted and managed directly by Google Firebase Authentication. We never store or have access to plaintext passwords.
  • Google Account Data: If you use "Continue with Google", we receive your authenticated name, email address, and profile photo authorized through Google OAuth.

B. Learning Progress & Activity Data

  • Exam & Practice Results: Question answers, simulated driving exam scores, completed categories (e.g., Road Signs, Right of Way, Road Safety), and Leitner spaced-repetition flashcard levels.
  • Study Metrics: Time spent learning and exam readiness analytics saved to your account in Cloud Firestore.

C. Technical & Diagnostic Information

  • Device Information: Device model, Android OS version, and app version to optimize user interface rendering and compatibility.
  • Crash & Performance Logs: Anonymous diagnostic information used solely to detect runtime crashes, resolve bugs, and maintain app stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly for the following purposes:

  • App Functionality: To authenticate your account and sync your learning progress and exam results across multiple devices.
  • Customer Support: To resolve technical issues or assist you with account inquiries.
  • Security: To protect user accounts against unauthorized access and maintain app integrity.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

Powerdrive Learning relies on trusted third-party cloud services for backend functionality:

  • Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore): For secure user login and real-time database synchronization. All data is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS and at rest using AES-256 encryption.
  • Google Play Services: For official app installation, updates, and licensing verification.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Information is only processed through our cloud infrastructure providers (Google Cloud/Firebase) as necessary to operate the application, or when required by applicable law or governmental authority.

5. User Account & Data Deletion Rights

You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your Powerdrive Learning account and all associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

To permanently delete your account, learning history, and all stored data, please email our support desk:

Email: info@arishnaiotsolutions.com

Required Information: Please provide your registered account email address.

6. Security Practices

All communications between the Powerdrive Learning application and our backend servers are secured using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption. Your personal data is stored in secure Google Cloud data centers with strict access controls.

7. Children's Privacy

Powerdrive Learning is designed for learner drivers preparing for their driving exam (typically ages 16 and older). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove the account and data.
