Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 13, 2026

International Siddhashram Shakti Centre ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Siddhashram mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the Siddhashram mobile app.

About Siddhashram App

The Siddhashram mobile application is designed to connect devotees worldwide with H.H. Rajrajeshwar Guruji’s spiritual discourses, Ashram news releases, daily Arti schedules, community events, volunteer programs, and educational Tech Hub courses.

Information We Collect

1. Personal Information You Provide

We may collect personal details when you interact with certain features in the App, such as submitting contact messages, applying to be a volunteer, or registering for Tech Hub courses:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Address / Location Details (when provided voluntarily for volunteer registration or inquiries)

2. Push Notifications & Technical Data

To deliver real-time updates for Ashram events, news, and Guruji’s discourses, we collect:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens
  • Device model, operating system version, and language settings
  • App crash logs and performance metrics (via Firebase Crashlytics)

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for spiritual and community service purposes, including:

  • Processing your inquiries and contact form submissions
  • Reviewing and processing volunteer applications
  • Managing enrollment for Tech Hub and digital skills courses
  • Sending push notifications for upcoming events, daily Arti times, and news
  • Ensuring app stability, performance, and security

Information We Do Not Collect or Share

We respect your privacy and adhere to strict data protection standards:

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not collect precise real-time GPS location tracking.
  • We do not collect or store financial payment card details directly within the App.

Third-Party Services

The App uses trusted Google Firebase services to operate securely:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: To manage user accounts securely.
  • Cloud Firestore & Firebase Storage: To store app content and user submissions.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): To send push notification alerts.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: To monitor and fix app crashes.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption and security measures (HTTPS/TLS in transit and encrypted storage at rest) to protect your personal information against unauthorized access.

Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal data stored with us. If you wish to delete your account or stored submissions, please contact us at the email below.
