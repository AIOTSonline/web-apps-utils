Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 3, 2026

Arishna IoT Solutions ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Kids XR mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use Kids XR.

About Kids XR

Kids XR is an educational augmented reality (AR) application designed to help children learn through interactive 3D experiences involving animals, science, oceans, food, environmental topics, and other educational content.

The App is intended for a mixed audience, including children and adults.

Information We Collect

Camera Access

Kids XR requires access to your device camera in order to provide augmented reality experiences. The camera feed is used to detect surfaces and place educational AR content within the user's environment.

Camera images and video are processed on the device and are not uploaded, stored, or shared by Arishna IoT Solutions.

Analytics Information

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App, improve educational experiences, fix issues, and enhance performance.

Analytics information may include:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Language settings
  • Usage statistics
  • Session information
  • Crash and diagnostic information

This information is collected in an aggregated form and is not used to directly identify individual users.

Advertising Information

Kids XR may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process certain device and usage information in accordance with Google's privacy practices.

For more information about how Google handles data, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

Information We Do Not Collect

At this time, Kids XR does not:

  • Require user registration or login
  • Collect names, addresses, or phone numbers
  • Collect precise location information
  • Collect photographs for storage
  • Upload camera images or videos to our servers
  • Sell personal information

Internet Usage

The App uses an internet connection to:

  • Download educational 3D assets and content updates
  • Support Firebase services
  • Support AdMob advertisements
  • Improve app reliability and performance

Storage and Cache

Kids XR may temporarily store downloaded educational assets and application data within your device's cache to improve performance and reduce repeated downloads.

The App does not request storage permissions from users for this purpose.

Children's Privacy

Protecting children's privacy is important to us. Kids XR is designed to provide educational experiences for young learners while respecting applicable privacy requirements.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise children's use of mobile devices and applications.

Third-Party Services

Kids XR may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services

These services operate under their own privacy policies and data handling practices.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the App evolves or legal requirements change. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

Early Access Notice

Kids XR is currently available in Early Access. Features, content, and functionality may change as development continues. This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect future improvements and new features.
